Typical situations

You may not need another tool. You may need clarity.

These are the kinds of situations where I am usually most useful.

01

Your SAC tenant has become difficult to govern.

Unused stories, duplicates, unclear ownership, inconsistent release controls or migration risk are creating technical debt.

02

You are preparing for a migration or platform change.

Not everything should move. You need a structured way to decide what to retain, retire, redesign or validate.

03

Business and technical teams disagree on the data.

KPI logic, mappings, requirements or source definitions are unclear and delivery is slowing down.

04

The programme is moving, but ownership is fragmented.

Dependencies, suppliers, releases and decisions need stronger governance without adding unnecessary bureaucracy.

05

You are evaluating Datasphere or BDC.

You need practical positioning around where the new capability fits — without pretending the current landscape does not exist.

06

The platform works, but adoption is poor.

Users do not trust what they see, cannot find the right content or still work around the platform in Excel.

How I work

No theatre. No mystery. No unnecessary transformation programme.

01

Understand before prescribing

I do not start with the platform. I start with the problem, the operating context and the people who have to live with the solution.

02

Make ownership visible

Clear decisions, responsibilities and escalation paths usually solve more than another governance deck.

03

Protect what already works

Modernisation should preserve useful business logic and knowledge rather than recreate it because a new platform exists.

04

Keep adoption in scope

A technically correct solution that people avoid is still an unsuccessful solution.
